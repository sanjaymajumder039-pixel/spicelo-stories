🍰 Cake Recipe

⭐ 4.9 Rating ⏱️ 60 Minutes 👨‍🍳 Medium 🍽️ 8 Servings

Ingredients

  • 2 Cups All-Purpose Flour
  • 1½ Cups Sugar
  • ½ Cup Butter
  • 2 Eggs
  • 1 Cup Milk
  • 2 tsp Baking Powder
  • 1 tsp Vanilla Essence
  • A Pinch of Salt
  • Chocolate Chips (Optional)
  • Whipped Cream for Decoration

Cooking Steps

  1. Preheat the oven to 180°C.
  2. Mix flour, baking powder, and salt.
  3. Beat butter and sugar until smooth.
  4. Add eggs one at a time and mix well.
  5. Add milk and vanilla essence.
  6. Slowly mix the dry ingredients into the batter.
  7. Pour the batter into a greased cake tin.
  8. Bake for 35–40 minutes.
  9. Cool the cake and decorate with whipped cream.
  10. Slice and serve.
