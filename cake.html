Easy Homemade Vanilla Cake Recipe

Learn how to make a soft, fluffy and delicious homemade vanilla cake with simple ingredients. This easy cake recipe is perfect for beginners and can be enjoyed with family and friends.

About This Homemade Vanilla Cake Recipe

This homemade vanilla cake is a simple and delicious recipe made with everyday ingredients such as flour, sugar, butter, eggs and milk. The recipe is designed to help beginners prepare a soft and fluffy cake at home using easy step-by-step instructions.

You can enjoy this cake as a simple homemade dessert or decorate it with whipped cream and chocolate chips for a more special presentation.

Ingredients for Homemade Vanilla Cake

  • 2 Cups All-Purpose Flour
  • 1½ Cups Sugar
  • ½ Cup Butter
  • 2 Eggs
  • 1 Cup Milk
  • 2 tsp Baking Powder
  • 1 tsp Vanilla Essence
  • A Pinch of Salt
  • Chocolate Chips (Optional)
  • Whipped Cream for Decoration

Make sure all ingredients are fresh and measured properly for a soft and fluffy homemade cake.

How to Make Homemade Vanilla Cake

Follow these simple step-by-step instructions to prepare a soft and fluffy homemade vanilla cake.

  1. Preheat the oven to 180°C.
  2. Cream the butter and sugar together until smooth.
  3. Add the eggs and vanilla essence and mix well.
  4. Add flour, baking powder and salt gradually.
  5. Add milk and mix until you get a smooth batter.
  6. Fold in chocolate chips if desired.
  7. Pour the batter into a prepared cake tin.
  8. Bake for about 30 minutes or until the cake is fully cooked.
  9. Let the cake cool and decorate with whipped cream.
  10. Serve and enjoy.

Tips for a Soft and Fluffy Cake

  • Make sure the butter and eggs are at room temperature before mixing.
  • Do not overmix the cake batter after adding the flour.
  • Preheat the oven properly before baking the cake.
  • Check the cake with a toothpick before removing it from the oven.
  • Allow the cake to cool completely before decorating it with whipped cream.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I make this cake without an oven?

Yes, you can make this cake using a heavy-bottomed pan or cooker, but the cooking time and method may be different.

How do I know when the cake is fully baked?

Insert a toothpick into the center of the cake. If it comes out clean, the cake is ready.

Can I store the cake for later?

Yes. Store the cake in an airtight container. It can usually be kept at room temperature for a short time or refrigerated for longer storage.

Can I add chocolate chips to this cake?

Yes, chocolate chips are optional and can be added to the batter before baking.
