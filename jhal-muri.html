Jhal Muri

A simple 10-minute snack with a personal story from my study days to my computer-work days.

The Story Behind This Dish

When I was studying, my mother used to make Jhal Muri for me. I would keep it beside my books while studying and writing, and eat it little by little.

Today, when I work on my computer, I sometimes make Jhal Muri and keep it beside me while I work. The difference is simple: earlier my mother made it for me, and now I make it myself.

For me, Jhal Muri is more than a quick snack. It is a small food memory that connects my study days with my life today.

Ready In

10 Minutes

Best Time

Morning or Evening

Type

Quick Snack

Ingredients

  • 250 g Muri (puffed rice)
  • 1 medium onion, finely chopped
  • 1 spoon mustard oil
  • 5 cashews
  • 5 raisins
  • 1 green chilli, finely chopped
  • 10 almonds
  • 2 small portions of fresh coriander leaves

How to Make Jhal Muri

  1. Take 250 g of muri in a mixing bowl.
  2. Add the finely chopped onion and green chilli.
  3. Add cashews, raisins and almonds.
  4. Add the fresh coriander leaves.
  5. Add one spoon of mustard oil.
  6. Mix everything well.
  7. Serve immediately and enjoy.

My Food Note

I especially enjoy Jhal Muri in the morning or evening. These days, it is also a simple snack I can prepare while working on my computer.
