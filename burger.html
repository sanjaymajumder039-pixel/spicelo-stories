🍔 Burger Recipe

⭐ 4.8 Rating ⏱️ 15 Minutes 👨‍🍳 Easy 🍽️ 2 Servings

Ingredients

  • Burger Buns
  • Chicken or Veg Patty
  • Cheese Slice
  • Lettuce
  • Tomato
  • Onion
  • Mayonnaise
  • Ketchup

Cooking Steps

  1. Toast the burger buns lightly.
  2. Cook the chicken or veg patty until golden.
  3. Spread mayonnaise and ketchup on the buns.
  4. Add lettuce, tomato, onion, cheese, and the patty.
  5. Close the burger and serve hot with fries.
