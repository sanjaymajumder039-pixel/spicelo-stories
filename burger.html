🍔 Burger Recipe

⏱️ 25 min 🍽️ 2 Servings 📊 Easy

Ingredients

  • Burger Buns
  • Chicken or Veg Patty
  • Cheese Slice
  • Lettuce
  • Tomato
  • Onion
  • Mayonnaise
  • Ketchup

Cooking Steps

  1. Toast the burger buns lightly.
  2. Cook the chicken or veg patty until fully cooked and golden.
  3. Place a cheese slice on the hot patty.
  4. Spread mayonnaise and ketchup on the burger buns.
  5. Add lettuce, tomato and onion.
  6. Place the cheesy patty inside the bun.
  7. Close the burger and serve hot.
