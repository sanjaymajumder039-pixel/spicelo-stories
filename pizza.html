🍕 Pizza Recipe

Ingredients

  • Pizza Base
  • Cheese
  • Tomato Sauce
  • Onion
  • Capsicum
  • Olives

Cooking Steps

  2. Add vegetables.

  4. Bake for 15 minutes.
