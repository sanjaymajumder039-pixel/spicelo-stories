🍗 Chicken Biryani Recipe

Learn how to make a delicious and aromatic homemade Chicken Biryani with basmati rice, tender chicken, yogurt and flavorful Indian spices. This easy step-by-step recipe is perfect for enjoying a special meal with family and friends.

About This Chicken Biryani Recipe

Chicken Biryani is a popular and flavorful Indian rice dish made by combining fragrant basmati rice with marinated chicken, yogurt, onions and aromatic spices. The layers of rice and chicken are cooked together to create a rich and delicious meal.

This homemade Chicken Biryani recipe uses simple ingredients and clear step-by-step instructions, making it easier to prepare a flavorful biryani at home.

Ingredients for Chicken Biryani

  • 1 kg Chicken
  • 1 kg Basmati Rice
  • 4 Large Onions, Thinly Sliced
  • 3 Tomatoes, Chopped
  • 1 Cup Yogurt
  • 2 tbsp Ginger-Garlic Paste
  • 2 tbsp Biryani Masala
  • 4–5 Green Chillies
  • Fresh Coriander, Chopped
  • Cooking Oil as Required
  • Salt to Taste

How to Make Chicken Biryani

  1. Wash the basmati rice thoroughly and soak it in water for about 20–30 minutes.
  2. Marinate the chicken with yogurt, ginger-garlic paste, biryani masala, green chillies and salt. Mix well and allow the chicken to absorb the flavours.
  3. Heat cooking oil in a large pot and fry the sliced onions until they become light golden in colour.
  4. Add the chopped tomatoes and cook until they become soft.
  5. Add the marinated chicken and cook it with the onion and tomato mixture until the chicken is partially cooked and the spices are well combined.
  6. In another pot, cook the soaked basmati rice until it is partially cooked. Drain the excess water carefully.
  7. Layer the partially cooked basmati rice over the chicken mixture.
  8. Sprinkle fresh coriander over the rice and cover the pot tightly with a lid.
  9. Cook the biryani on low heat until the chicken is fully cooked and the rice becomes soft and fluffy.
  10. Gently mix the biryani, serve hot and enjoy it with your family or friends.

Tips for Making Delicious Chicken Biryani

  • Use good-quality basmati rice for a fragrant and fluffy biryani.
  • Do not overcook the rice before layering it with the chicken.
  • Marinate the chicken well so that the spices and flavors are absorbed properly.
  • Cook the biryani on low heat after layering to help the flavors combine.
  • Keep the pot tightly covered while cooking so that the steam remains inside.
  • Allow the biryani to rest for a few minutes before serving for better flavor.

Chicken Biryani FAQ

How long does it take to cook Chicken Biryani?

This Chicken Biryani recipe takes approximately 60 minutes, including preparation and cooking time.

Which rice is best for Chicken Biryani?

Good-quality basmati rice is commonly used because it becomes fragrant and fluffy when cooked properly.

Can I prepare Chicken Biryani for a family picnic?

Yes. Chicken Biryani can be prepared in a large quantity and is a great meal to enjoy with family and friends during a picnic or special gathering.

How can I prevent the biryani rice from becoming mushy?

Avoid fully cooking the rice before layering it with the chicken. Cook it only partially, then finish cooking it on low heat during the final layering process.
