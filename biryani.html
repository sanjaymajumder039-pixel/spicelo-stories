🍗 Chicken Biryani Recipe

⏱️
Prep Time

20 min

🔥
Cook Time

40 min

🍽️
Servings

4

📊
Difficulty

Medium

Ingredients

  • Chicken
  • Basmati Rice
  • Onion
  • Tomato
  • Yogurt
  • Ginger-Garlic Paste
  • Biryani Masala
  • Green Chilli
  • Fresh Coriander
  • Cooking Oil

Cooking Steps

  1. Wash and soak the basmati rice.
  2. Marinate the chicken with yogurt and spices.
  3. Cook the marinated chicken with onion, tomato and spices.
  4. Cook the rice until it is partially done.
  5. Layer the rice over the chicken mixture.
  6. Cover and cook on low heat until the rice is fully cooked.
  7. Garnish with fresh coriander and serve hot.
