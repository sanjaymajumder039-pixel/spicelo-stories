🍗 Chicken Biryani Recipe

⭐ 4.9 Rating ⏱️ 45 Minutes 👨‍🍳 Medium 🍽️ 4 Servings

Ingredients

  • 500g Chicken
  • 2 Cups Basmati Rice
  • 2 Onions (Sliced)
  • 2 Tomatoes (Chopped)
  • 1 Cup Yogurt
  • Ginger-Garlic Paste
  • Biryani Masala
  • Fresh Coriander & Mint Leaves
  • Saffron Milk (Optional)
  • Salt & Oil

Cooking Steps

  1. Wash and soak the basmati rice for 30 minutes.
  2. Marinate the chicken with yogurt, ginger-garlic paste, spices, and salt.
  3. Fry the onions until golden brown.
  4. Cook the marinated chicken with tomatoes and spices.
  5. Half-cook the rice separately.
  6. Layer the rice over the cooked chicken.
  7. Top with fried onions, mint, coriander, and saffron milk.
  8. Cover and cook on low heat for 20–25 minutes.
  9. Serve hot with raita or salad.
