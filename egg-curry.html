Egg Curry

A simple homemade Egg Curry for two, inspired by a childhood tiffin memory with my grandmother.

The Story Behind This Dish

When I was going to school, my grandmother used to make this Egg Curry for me and pack it in my tiffin.

During tiffin time, I would eat it with white rice. It was a simple meal, but it became a special food memory from my school days.

Today, I make the same kind of Egg Curry myself and enjoy it with my meal. The memory of my grandmother is still connected with this recipe.

Ready In

25 Minutes

Serves

2 Persons

Type

Main Course

Ingredients

For 2 persons

  • 4 eggs
  • 4 green chillies
  • 1 tomato
  • 2 onions
  • Salt to taste
  • 250 ml water
  • ½ spoon turmeric powder
  • 4 spoons ginger and garlic paste
  • 2 spoons mustard oil

🎬 Egg Curry Video

How to Make Egg Curry

  1. First, prepare the ingredients. Cut the tomato and onions and wash the ingredients before cooking.
  2. Boil 4 eggs.
  3. Add a little turmeric to the boiled eggs and fry them in mustard oil. Once fried, keep the eggs aside.
  4. Heat a kadai and add 2 spoons of mustard oil.
  5. When the oil becomes hot, add the onions and salt. Fry them for some time.
  6. Add the prepared tomato, green chillies and ginger-garlic paste.
  7. Fry everything on medium flame until the mixture becomes brown.
  8. Add 250 ml water and let the gravy cook.
  9. Finally, add the fried eggs to the gravy.
  10. Cook until the Egg Curry is ready.

Total time: approximately 25 minutes.

My Food Note

For me, this Egg Curry is not only a recipe. It carries a memory of my school days, my grandmother, and the tiffin I used to eat with white rice.

Today I make it myself, but the food memory remains the same.
