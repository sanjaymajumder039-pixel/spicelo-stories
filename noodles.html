🍜 Noodles Recipe

⭐ 4.7 Rating ⏱️ 20 Minutes 👨‍🍳 Easy 🍽️ 2 Servings

Ingredients

  • 200g Noodles
  • 1 Carrot (Julienned)
  • 1 Capsicum (Sliced)
  • 1 Onion (Sliced)
  • 2 Garlic Cloves (Chopped)
  • 2 tbsp Soy Sauce
  • 1 tbsp Chili Sauce
  • 1 tbsp Tomato Ketchup
  • Salt & Black Pepper
  • 2 tbsp Cooking Oil

Cooking Steps

  1. Boil the noodles until just cooked, then drain.
  2. Heat oil in a pan and sauté the garlic.
  3. Add onion, carrot, and capsicum.
  4. Cook the vegetables for 3–4 minutes.
  5. Add soy sauce, chili sauce, and ketchup.
  6. Mix in the boiled noodles.
  7. Add salt and black pepper to taste.
  8. Toss everything well for 2–3 minutes.
  9. Serve hot with your favorite sauce.
