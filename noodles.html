🍜 Noodles Recipe

⏱️ 25 min 🍽️ 2 Servings 📊 Easy

Ingredients

  • 200g Noodles
  • 1 Carrot (Julienned)
  • 1 Capsicum (Sliced)
  • 1 Onion (Sliced)
  • 2 Garlic Cloves (Chopped)
  • 2 tbsp Soy Sauce
  • 1 tbsp Chili Sauce
  • 1 tbsp Tomato Ketchup
  • Salt & Black Pepper
  • 2 tbsp Cooking Oil

Cooking Steps

  1. Boil the noodles according to the package instructions and drain them.
  2. Heat cooking oil in a pan and sauté the chopped garlic.
  3. Add onion, carrot and capsicum and stir-fry the vegetables.
  4. Add soy sauce, chili sauce and tomato ketchup.
  5. Add the cooked noodles and toss everything together.
  6. Season with salt and black pepper.
  7. Cook for a few minutes and serve hot.
