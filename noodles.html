🍜 Easy Homemade Egg Noodles Recipe

Learn how to make easy and delicious homemade egg noodles with fresh vegetables, eggs and flavorful sauces. This simple recipe is perfect for a quick meal or for preparing a tasty homemade tiffin.

About This Homemade Egg Noodles Recipe

Noodles are one of my favorite foods, and I enjoy making them at home whenever I get the chance. I like preparing simple homemade noodles with eggs, fresh vegetables and flavorful sauces.

Sometimes, when I go to the office, I prepare homemade egg noodles and take them with me as my tiffin. I usually make enough for two people and enjoy eating this simple homemade meal. For me, noodles are not just a quick meal—they are one of my favorite foods to cook and enjoy.

Ingredients

  • 200g Noodles
  • 2 Eggs
  • 1 Small Carrot, Julienned
  • 1 Small Capsicum, Sliced
  • 1 Small Onion, Sliced
  • 2 Garlic Cloves, Chopped
  • 2 Tablespoons Soy Sauce
  • 1 Tablespoon Chili Sauce
  • 1 Tablespoon Tomato Ketchup
  • Salt, to taste
  • Black Pepper, to taste
  • 2 Tablespoons Cooking Oil

Cooking Steps

  1. Bring water to a boil in a large pot. Add the noodles and cook according to the package instructions until just tender. Drain the noodles and set them aside.
  2. Heat a little oil in a large pan or wok on medium heat. Crack the eggs into the pan and scramble them until fully cooked. Remove them from the pan and keep aside.
  3. Add the remaining cooking oil to the same pan. Add the chopped garlic and sauté for a few seconds until fragrant.
  4. Add the sliced onion, julienned carrot and sliced capsicum. Stir-fry the vegetables on medium-high heat until they are cooked but still slightly crisp.
  5. Add the soy sauce, chili sauce and tomato ketchup. Mix everything well with the vegetables.
  6. Add the cooked noodles and scrambled eggs to the pan. Toss everything gently until the noodles are evenly coated with the sauces.
  7. Add salt and black pepper according to your taste. Mix well and cook for another 1–2 minutes.
  8. Serve the homemade egg noodles hot and fresh, or pack them as a tasty homemade tiffin.

Tips for Making Delicious Egg Noodles

  • Do not overcook the noodles, as they can become soft and sticky after stir-frying.
  • Drain the cooked noodles properly before adding them to the pan.
  • Cook the eggs separately first so they remain evenly mixed with the noodles.
  • Stir-fry the vegetables on medium-high heat to keep them slightly crisp.
  • Prepare all ingredients before starting because stir-frying noodles happens quickly.
  • Do not add too much sauce, as it can make the noodles overly salty or soggy.
  • Serve immediately for the best taste, or allow the noodles to cool slightly before packing them as tiffin.

Egg Noodles FAQ

Can I make egg noodles at home?

Yes. This homemade egg noodles recipe can be prepared easily with noodles, eggs, fresh vegetables and simple sauces.

How many people does this recipe serve?

This recipe is designed to serve approximately 2 people.

How can I prevent noodles from becoming sticky?

Avoid overcooking the noodles, drain them properly and toss them gently with the other ingredients while stir-frying.

Can I pack these egg noodles as tiffin?

Yes. You can prepare the noodles at home, allow them to cool slightly and pack them in a suitable food container for tiffin.
