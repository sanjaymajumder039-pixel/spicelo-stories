Privacy Policy

Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how SpiceLo Stories handles information when you visit and interact with our website.

1. About This Website

SpiceLo Stories is a food and recipe website created by Sanjay Majumdar. The website shares recipes, food stories, cooking information and related content.

Website: https://spicelostories.com

2. Information We May Receive

When you use certain features of SpiceLo Stories, you may voluntarily provide information such as your name and the comments you choose to post on recipe pages.

We do not ask visitors to provide sensitive personal information through the website.

3. Comments

SpiceLo Stories allows visitors to post comments on recipe pages.

When you submit a comment, the name and comment text you provide may be stored so that the comment can be displayed on the website.

Please do not include passwords, financial information, private contact details or other sensitive information in public comments.

4. Likes and Browser Storage

SpiceLo Stories provides a Like feature for recipes. To help prevent repeated likes from the same browser, the website may store a randomly generated visitor identifier in your browser's local storage.

This identifier is not intended to identify you by name, email address or other directly identifying information.

5. Supabase

SpiceLo Stories currently uses Supabase as a backend service for certain website features, including recipe Likes and Comments.

Information submitted through these features may be processed and stored through Supabase services.

6. Cookies and Similar Technologies

SpiceLo Stories may use browser storage or similar technologies to provide and maintain website functionality.

SpiceLo Stories uses Google Analytics to understand website traffic, page views and visitor engagement. Google Analytics may collect information such as page views, events, device and browser information, and other usage data according to Google's services and policies.

SpiceLo Stories is not currently displaying Google AdSense advertisements.

If analytics, advertising or other third-party technologies are added in the future, this Privacy Policy will be updated to explain their relevant data practices.

7. Google Analytics

SpiceLo Stories uses Google Analytics, a web analytics service provided by Google, to understand how visitors use the website.

Google Analytics may collect information such as page views, events, device and browser information, approximate location information and other usage data. This information helps us understand website traffic and improve the user experience.

Google Analytics uses cookies and similar technologies to collect and measure website usage information. The use of Google Analytics is subject to Google's own privacy practices and policies.

8. Advertising

SpiceLo Stories is not currently displaying Google AdSense advertisements.

If advertising services are introduced in the future, this Privacy Policy will be updated with the relevant information about advertising technologies, cookies, third-party vendors and available privacy choices.

9. Third-Party Services and Links

The website may use third-party services to provide functionality, hosting, fonts, backend services or other technical features.

Third-party websites or services may have their own privacy policies and terms. SpiceLo Stories is not responsible for the privacy practices of external websites that are not controlled by us.

10. Data Security

We take reasonable steps to protect information handled through the website. However, no method of transmission or electronic storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

11. Children's Privacy

SpiceLo Stories is a general-audience food website and is not specifically directed toward children.

We do not knowingly request sensitive personal information from children through the website.

12. Your Choices

You can choose not to submit a comment or other optional information to the website.

You can also manage or delete information stored in your browser through your browser's settings.

13. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time when website features, services or legal requirements change.

Any updated version will be published on this page.
